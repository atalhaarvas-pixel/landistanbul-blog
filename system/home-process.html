Frequently asked questions

Can foreigners buy property in Turkey?

Yes. Citizens of most countries can buy property in Turkey in their own name. Some areas, such as military zones, are closed to foreign buyers; we check this for every property before you commit.

Can I buy without coming to Turkey?

Yes. Many of our clients buy from abroad: we send video viewings, and the purchase can be completed by a lawyer acting for you under a notarised power of attorney.

How much do I need for Turkish citizenship?

Buying property worth at least $400,000 and keeping it for 3 years qualifies you and your family to apply. Check your eligibility in 60 seconds →

Can I get residence without buying a home?

Yes. Since November 2026 foreign investors can get a 3-year residence permit with $150,000 (5 years with $200,000) placed in a Turkish bank deposit, government bonds, an approved fund or a private pension. Read the guide →
