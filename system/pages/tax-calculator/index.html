How the tax is calculated

Turkey taxes the gain on a property sold within five years of acquisition (Income Tax Law, repeated Article 80). The calculation is always in Turkish lira, with the purchase cost adjusted for inflation:

  1. Convert to lira at the right datesPurchase price at the purchase-date rate, sale price at the sale-date rate.
  2. Index the purchase costMultiply by Yİ-ÜFE of the month before the sale ÷ Yİ-ÜFE of the month before the purchase, if the increase is 10% or more.
  3. Deduct selling costsThe seller's 2% title deed fee and other documented costs of the sale.
  4. Net gainSale price minus indexed cost minus costs. Zero or less means no tax.
  5. Annual exemption₺150,000 for sales in 2026, once per calendar year for all your property gains.
  6. Progressive taxThe non-wage income tariff of the year of sale, from 15% to 40%.

5 years = no tax

Sell five years or more after the date on your title deed and the gain is not taxed, whatever the amount.

Inherited or gift

Property received free of charge (inheritance or gift) is outside this tax, even if sold within five years.

Declare in March

A taxable gain is declared on the income tax return in March of the year after the sale and paid in two instalments.

Questions sellers ask

Do foreigners pay capital gains tax when selling property in Turkey?

Yes, if the property is sold within 5 years of acquisition and the inflation-indexed gain is above the annual exemption (₺150,000 for sales in 2026). The rules are the same for Turkish citizens and foreigners. Property held 5 years or more, and property received by inheritance or as a gift, is not taxed on the gain.

Which tax rates apply to property gains in 2026?

Gains from selling property are non-wage income, so the 2026 non-wage tariff applies: 15% up to ₺190,000, 20% up to ₺400,000, 27% up to ₺1,000,000, 35% up to ₺5,300,000 and 40% above that.

I paid in US dollars. Which exchange rate is used?

The tax is calculated in Turkish lira, so the purchase price is converted at the rate on the purchase date (the rate on your bank's foreign-exchange purchase certificate, DAB, is the best figure) and the sale price at the rate on the sale date. Converting both at today's rate gives a wrong result.

How is the purchase price indexed for inflation?

The purchase cost is multiplied by the ratio of the domestic producer price index (Yİ-ÜFE) of the month before the sale to that of the month before the purchase, provided the increase is at least 10%.

I got citizenship with this property. Can I sell after 3 years tax-free?

The 3-year citizenship lock and the 5-year tax rule are separate. Selling in years 3 to 5 is allowed, but the indexed gain above the exemption is taxed. Use the calculator to compare selling now with waiting until the 5-year date.

Is the title deed fee affected by the 5-year rule?

No. The title deed fee (4% of the declared price, 2% paid by the seller and 2% by the buyer) is a transaction fee paid on every sale. The seller's share can be deducted as an expense when the gain is taxable.
